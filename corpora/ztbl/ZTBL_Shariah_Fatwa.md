# ZTBL Islamic Banking Division – Shariah Fatwa

Muhammad ﷺ — Peace and blessings be upon him

This is to certify that the Islamic Banking Division of Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited (ZTBL) operates under the supervision of qualified and renowned Shariah scholars and provides Shariah-compliant investment and other banking services. For this purpose, it has also been duly licensed by the Islamic Banking Department of the State Bank of Pakistan.

The Islamic Banking Division of Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited offers financing on the basis of Qarz-e-Hasna (benevolent loan), under which no kind of profit is charged or paid. In the case of Savings Accounts, based on the principles of Mudarabah, and Term Deposits, the relationship between the bank and the account holders is based on Mudarabah, which is permissible according to Shariah.

The Islamic Banking Division of Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited accepts deposits and provides financing to customers under different Shariah-compliant arrangements. All transactions are carried out under the supervision of the Shariah Board, and every effort is made to ensure that the principles and requirements of Islamic banking are observed. The Shariah Board reviews and approves the products, procedures, and agreements of the Islamic Banking Division, and their compliance with Shariah is ensured.

Therefore, the undersigned certify that, under the current structure, no transaction/account of the Islamic Banking Division of Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited is contrary to Shariah principles. Any clarification regarding this matter may be obtained from the undersigned.

And Allah knows best.
